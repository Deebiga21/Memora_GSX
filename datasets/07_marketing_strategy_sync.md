# 07. Marketing Strategy Sync

Date: October 5, 2026
Project: Campus Smart Mobility Initiative
Lead: Priya Sharma

## 1. Objective
Discuss the promotional campaign for the rollout of the new NovaTech Mobility App to ensure high student and staff adoption rates.

## 2. Key Channels
- **Campus Email Newsletters:** Weekly updates starting one month before launch.
- **Social Media:** Teaser campaigns on the official university Instagram and Twitter accounts.
- **Physical Banners:** Placed at high-traffic areas like the student union, library, and main dining halls.
- **Orientation Events:** Live demos during the Winter term orientation.

## 3. Incentives
- Early adopters who download the app and complete a feedback survey will be entered into a raffle for campus store vouchers.

## 4. Action Items
- Draft the initial email newsletter and get approval from the communications office.
- Design the physical banners and send them to the print shop.
- Finalize the rules and budget for the incentive raffle.
